Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device.

Effective date: [1 September 2026]  ·  Developer: mwbp.io

Overview

toluTag is a utility for interacting with toluTag NFC hardware (NXP SE05x secure elements). It lets you create keys, sign messages, and read and write data on your own tags over NFC.

toluTag is private by default. The app has no servers, no user accounts, and does not transmit your data anywhere. Everything the app does happens locally on your iPhone and on the tag you tap.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We (mwbp.io) have no back-end, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. We never see, receive, or store any data from your use of the app.

Information stored on your device

To provide its features, toluTag stores the following only on your device, in the iOS Keychain (encrypted at rest by iOS):

  • Tag records you choose to save — labels, object identifiers, public keys, Ethereum addresses, saved text, card unique identifiers, card names, and collected-tag lists.
  • PINs you enter for PIN-protected keys, if you choose to save a record that uses one.

This information:

  • never leaves your device except when you explicitly export or share it (for example, using the iOS share sheet to save a collection as a JSON file, or copying a value to your clipboard);
  • is protected behind Face ID / Touch ID / device passcode before your saved records can be viewed;
  • is removed when you delete the record in the app or delete the app from your device.

Device permissions we use

  • NFC (Near Field Communication) — used to read from and write to toluTag hardware when you initiate a scan. NFC is only active while you are performing an action in the app.
  • Face ID / biometrics — used locally to unlock your saved tag records. Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS and Apple's Secure Enclave; the app never accesses or stores it.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera, and does not use background activity.

Blockchain and cryptographic data

toluTag can derive Ethereum addresses and produce cryptographic signatures locally using keys held on your tag's secure element. These operations are performed on your device and on the tag. The app does not broadcast transactions, connect to any blockchain network, or send signatures anywhere — any resulting data (such as a signature or exported list) is yours to use or share as you choose.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your information, because we do not collect or receive any. Data leaves your device only through actions you take yourself (exporting a file, sharing, or copying), and only to the destination you select.

Children's privacy

toluTag is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
